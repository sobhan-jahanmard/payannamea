# Editing review

Use the original DOCX and order notes as the reference. Compare meaning,
spelling, grammar, punctuation, readability, and consistency in the revised
text. Check that data, citations, quotations, tables, and formatting remain
faithful. Flag changes that exceed the requested scope or leave a material
error unresolved. Check each final PDF page and each opening sample page
against the native Word files and the rounded-up 20% rule.

For the existing rubric, score the brief and editorial depth under content,
meaning and fact preservation under source fidelity, grammar and readability
under narrative clarity, typography under visual design, and Word/PDF integrity
and excerpt fidelity under technical delivery. Cite page or paragraph locations
for every issue.
