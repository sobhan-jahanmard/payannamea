# Thesis proposal decisions

## Proposal boundary

The output is a research proposal, not a finished thesis. Present a concrete,
sourced problem and a feasible plan. Complete the literature comparison, gap
argument, method design and feasibility assessment now. Use prospective language
for planned recruitment, data collection, experiments, implementation and expected
results. Do not invent completed findings, significance tests or ethical approval.

Read the customer's abstract/problem description carefully. Keep questions,
objectives, hypotheses when appropriate, variables/concepts, data needs and
proposed analysis aligned. A hypothesis is not mandatory for every qualitative
or exploratory design. Confirm a consequential ambiguity while progressing on
independent literature and formatting work.

## Structure and substantive work

The university proposal form takes precedence. A fallback is title/cover; problem
and importance; critical literature and gap; objectives/questions/hypotheses;
conceptual framework; methodology; feasibility/resources/ethics; work plan;
expected contributions and limitations; references.

Describe population/corpus, sampling/selection, instruments, operational
definitions, analysis procedure and appropriate validity/reliability checks.
Justify sample size or state what information is still needed to set it.
For desk research explain search strategy, eligibility, comparison and synthesis.
For computational/design studies name inputs, baselines and evaluation criteria.

Use an editable timetable or Gantt-style plan with phases, dependencies and
credible durations. Add a budget only when requested or needed for feasibility;
unknown costs remain labelled estimates. Include risks and mitigation.
Show required approval/consent/privacy procedures as pending requirements,
not approvals already obtained. A small design example may illustrate feasibility
when labelled synthetic and permitted; it is never an empirical finding.

## Acceptance checks

- Literature supports the problem, gap and need for the proposed project.
- Every question maps to a feasible collection/analysis procedure and expected output.
- Timetable, access, resources, ethics and key risks are coherent.
- Expected contributions are distinguished from completed outcomes throughout.
- The exact proposal form and requested quantity are respected; no artificial results chapter is added.
- Full/sample Word/PDF and required form/appendix companions are current and visually reviewed.
