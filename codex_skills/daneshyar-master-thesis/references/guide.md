# Master's thesis decisions

## Scope and structure

Read the research question, discipline, methodology, advisor instructions and
university template. Establish what contribution the evidence permits: empirical
finding, method comparison, design evaluation, theoretical argument or structured
synthesis. Do not claim novelty from an unverified absence of publications.
The required depth comes from critical reasoning and validation, not longer prose.

A fallback structure is abstract/front matter; problem and contribution;
critical literature/gap; methodology and research design; analysis/results;
discussion/validation; conclusion, limits and future work; references/appendices.
Follow the actual template if it differs. Cover metadata and academic degree must
match this order. Do not import undergraduate cover wording or unrelated factory
deliverables.

## Method and evidence

Make an explicit question-to-method-to-result map. Explain why the selected method
fits the question, what alternatives were considered, and the study's assumptions.
For quantitative work record real data origin, preprocessing, sample definition,
units, model parameters, uncertainty and suitable checks/baselines. Report causal
or statistical conclusions only when the design supports them.
For qualitative work use real source material, selection/coding criteria and
traceable interpretive evidence; do not fabricate interviews or quotations.
For theoretical/review work state search/selection/comparison logic and its limits.

Keep executed scripts and data under research/ when applicable. Repeat key
calculations, test sensitivity or triangulate interpretations where the method
requires it. Report inconvenient results and uncertainty. If access to essential
data, fieldwork or approval is missing, retain valid work and state the blocker;
do not turn a plan into an alleged completed master's study.

## Acceptance checks

- A supported gap leads to a precise question and justified contribution.
- The method is actually applied, with traceable data and reproducible intermediate steps.
- Validation, uncertainty and alternative explanations receive discipline-appropriate treatment.
- The discussion compares findings with real cited literature without overstating novelty.
- The abstract/conclusion reflect the executed work, and full/sample editable Word/PDF files pass current inspection.
