# Bachelor's thesis decisions

## Intake and structure

Use the registered title, faculty, advisor, degree, field and methodology. A
missing advisor name is a cover gap, never permission to invent a person. Apply
the uploaded institutional template before any default chapter sequence.

A useful fallback is front matter and abstract; problem/objectives; focused
literature and concepts; method/data; results and discussion; conclusion and
limitations; references and necessary appendices. Merge sections for small orders.
Do not assume every thesis is an industrial feasibility study. Factory-specific
market, equipment, layout and economics apply only to that topic or an explicit
guideline. If interim reports are required, assign their actual content and
titles in the plan rather than copying another order's deliverables.

## Research and execution

Define one bounded question and a method that the available evidence can answer.
A structured literature comparison, design calculation or documented case study
can be appropriate when the order permits it. Perform the comparison/calculation;
a chapter that only explains how it could be performed is unfinished.
Keep a table linking question, evidence, method and result.

Record dataset origin, units, dates, inclusion/exclusion decisions and limitations.
Any survey, interview, experiment or organizational measurement must come from
real supplied/authorized records. With no required empirical dataset, finish
independent literature/design work and explain the precise data blocker. Do not
substitute invented participants or simulated results for observations.

## Acceptance checks

- The abstract, question, executed method, results and conclusion describe the same project.
- Tables/figures and citations support the argument and can be reproduced from the recorded evidence.
- The conclusion answers the research question with claims proportionate to the evidence.
- Cover, TOC, captions, bibliography and appendices match the university requirements.
- Page/word quantity is met through substance; the full/sample Word/PDF package is current.
