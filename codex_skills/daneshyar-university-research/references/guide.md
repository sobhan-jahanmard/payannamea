# University research decisions

## Course context

Use course_name, instructor_name, degree, field, exact question and assignment
instructions to set depth. Missing course/instructor information is a material
context or cover gap, not a reason to infer a thesis advisor.
Use the course-report cover sequence: university/faculty if supplied, course,
title, student, instructor. Do not add degree-award wording, thesis defense pages
or thesis chapter labels unless the actual assignment explicitly requires them.

A compact default structure is abstract when useful; introduction and question;
focused background; method; findings/analysis; discussion/conclusion; references.
For short orders combine sections; for long reports use meaningful thematic
sections. Follow a supplied classroom rubric or template before this default.

## Research and analysis

Prefer sources directly answering the question, whether Iranian or international;
use a local geography only when specified or substantively needed. Do not impose
an unrelated Iranian-market feasibility study, financial statements, factory
layout or mandatory five-chapter thesis on a course assignment.

If asked to compare, define criteria and perform the comparison. If asked to
calculate, show inputs/units and execute it. If asked for a literature review,
synthesize agreements, differences and limitations rather than listing summaries.
A conceptual question can be answered by a structured, source-backed argument;
do not fabricate numeric data to fill a chart. A requested numeric chart needs
actual evidence or a clearly permitted labelled scenario.

## Acceptance checks

- The report answers the assigned question and remains tied to the course.
- Named methods are applied to real evidence and produce a concrete finding.
- Citations are accurate, references are real and any proxies/assumptions are visible.
- Figures support the argument and body text explains what they establish.
- Structure, language, quantity and cover match the course order; full/sample Word/PDF and explicitly required companions are complete.
